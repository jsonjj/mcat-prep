# Frozen public-claims policy

Until the corresponding validation gates pass (see `docs/validation/`), the product and all marketing/UI copy **must not** claim any of the following:

- A guaranteed or predicted MCAT score, or a score increase.
- A study-speed multiplier ("learn X% faster").
- Validated "mastery" as an external guarantee (internal mastery states are provisional evidence labels, clearly worded as such).
- Reduced test anxiety as a proven outcome.
- Any official AAMC affiliation, endorsement, or use of official AAMC items.

## Allowed, honest framing

- "Personalized study plan based on your evidence."
- "Readiness trend" (categorical/directional, with uncertainty) — never a scaled-score number before gates pass.
- "Provisional placement," "current evidence," "needs a fresh check."
- Explicit uncertainty: evidence counts, recency, and what would raise confidence.

## Naming

"MCAT Mastery" is the current working name (owner-approved for now). Trademark clearance and AAMC-affiliation review remain required before public brand launch. UI must not imply affiliation with the AAMC or the official MCAT program.
